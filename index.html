A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little more
clarity.

Who is the service for?+

Organizations looking to understand and support employee wellbeing, including educational institutions and other workplaces. The assessment purpose and participant group are agreed before the programme begins.

What does the assessment explore?+

The profile can explore emotional wellbeing, life satisfaction, self-esteem, positive functioning, relationships, personality preferences, reasoning, and personal reflections. A psychologist selects the relevant assessment mix for the agreed purpose.

How are psychologists involved?+

The service is built around professional interpretation. Software organizes responses, calculates scores, and assists report preparation; psychologists consider the evidence in context, prepare final reports, and guide appropriate follow-up.

Who receives the results?+

Recipients and confidentiality arrangements are agreed at the outset. Individual feedback and organizational summaries serve different purposes, with information sharing scoped to those agreed purposes.

What happens after the report?+

Depending on the person and circumstances, a psychologist may suggest a feedback session, further assessment, counselling referral, coping-skills work, or workplace support. Organizational findings can also inform wider wellbeing initiatives.
